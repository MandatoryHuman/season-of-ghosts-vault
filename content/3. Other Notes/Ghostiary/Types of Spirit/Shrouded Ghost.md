## Behaviour and Haunts
- While some Pocongs are passive, only manifesting to relay messages from beyond or ask for prayer, others take pleasure in frightening the living with their appearance.
- Pocongs are able to pass through solid objects and even teleport.
- Some Pocongs have been observed to form their own colonies with anywhere from dozens to thousands of members.

### Recognition Guide
- Pocongs resemble a person wrapped in a tradtional burial shroud. They mirror not just the appearance the person had in life, but also the decomposition of their body over time.
- Some reports claim Pocong hop to move, as the shroud is tied in a knot at their feet, preventing them from walking normally. Others claim that a real Pocong hovers above the ground, and that if you see one hopping it means it's a living person pretending to be a Pocong.

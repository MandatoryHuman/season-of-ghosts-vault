## Overview
![[Flat-Faced.webp|float-left|400]]Noppera-bō are a faceless species of spirit known for their tricks and deceptions. Their origin, as well as the reason for their pranks is unknown.

## Behaviour and Haunts
- Noppera-bō are said to appear to people by impersonating someone they know, before turning to reveal their face - or lack thereof.
- While known for frightening humans, Noppera-bō are generally harmless.
- Quite a number of Noppera-bō sightings have eventually been revealed to actually be cases of [[Kitsune]] pretending to be Noppera-bō via shapeshifting.

### Recognition Guide
- Aside from their face, Noppera-bō look identical to humans. Unfortunately, their shapeshifting abilities mean they are capable of looking identical to *any* human at *any* time.

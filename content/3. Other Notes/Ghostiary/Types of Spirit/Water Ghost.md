## Behaviour and Haunts
- These ghosts attempt to perform Tì Shēn. This means they attempt to drown living people in order to force them to take the ghost's place and free the spirit. They do this either by grabbing people who foolishly swim in their resting place, or by causing paralysis and cramps.

### Recognition Guide
- These spirits are rarely seen without being concealed withing murky waters, but reports of drownings may be indicative of their presence.

### Associated Hazards
- Drowning

## Exorcism and Resolution
- To exorcise these spirits, their physical remains must be dragged from the river or lake they are bound to. This breaks the binding and allows them to enter the afterlife.
- Alternatively, a priest can perform a ritual to prevent the spirit from interacting with the mortal world, or to guide them to the afterlife.

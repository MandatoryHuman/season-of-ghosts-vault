## Overview

![[z_assets/Creatures/Ghost Types/Yuki-onna.webp|float-left|400]]Yuki-onna are spirits that are capable of appearing and acting entirely human, however, they are in fact made of nothing but ice and snow.

## Behaviour and Haunts

- Most Yuki-onna are actually passive, with some attempting to integrate into mortal life; there are even stories of mortal men marrying a Yuki-onna, only to discover their true nature when she melts in the bath!
- Some Yuki-onna are known to appear on snowy nights and offer travellers a baby to hold, in a manner similar to a [[Maternal Ghost|Ubume]]. Just like with an Ubume, should the ghostly child be accepted, they will rapidly grow heavier and pin the mortal down, where hypothermia will finish them off.
  - Alternatively, failing to accept the child will cause the Yuki-onna to shove the poor traveller off a snowy valley, to their doom.

### Recognition Guide

- Yuki-onna are known for appearing so pale that in the snow, only their back hair and thin lips are visible.

### Associated Hazards

- Freezing

## Exorcism and Resolution

- Many communities known to be home to Yuki-onna simply warn their children not to go out to play on snowy days, and to avoid strangers.
- Stories do tell of Yuki-onna rewarding those who treat them with kindness. These rewards range from simply being allowed to live, to being given piles of gold.

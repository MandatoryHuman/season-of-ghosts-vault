## Overview
![[Turtle Ghost.webp|float-left|400]]Suppon no yūrei are the spirits of softshell turtles, a delicacy that is highly sought after. Their spirits linger after being eaten to seek revenge on those who ate them.

## Behaviour and Haunts
- Suppon no yūrei are vengeful spirits that attempt to get revenge on humans who either ate them, or aided in their catching, cooking, and sale.
- It's said that if a Suppon no yūrei ever manages to bite you it would never let go; while they are not necessarily the most dangerous type of spirit, they are certainly among the most tenacious.
- Aside from physical harm, Suppon no yūrei are also capable of possessing a person. In this case, the victim will begin to grow facial and bodily features that resemble those of a softshell turtle. In extreme cases, the mouth can change shape so greatly that a person can be unable to eat solid food.

### Recognition Guide
- These spirits are very large, with long legless bodies and pointed lips, mirroring those features of the turtles they originate as.

### Associated Hazards
- Biting
- Possession
- Body morphing

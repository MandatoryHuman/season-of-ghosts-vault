## Overview

Phi Am are spirits that haunt victims - mainly men - during the night.

## Behaviour and Haunts

- The Phi Am will sit on a victim's chest as they sleep, pinning them to their bed and making breathing difficult.

### Recognition Guide

- Phi Am generally appear as large and grotesque women.

### Associated Hazards

- Sleep-Paralysis
- Asphyxiation

## Exorcism and Resolution

- As Phi Am target men, it's said that wearing lipstick at night can trick them and render you immune to them.

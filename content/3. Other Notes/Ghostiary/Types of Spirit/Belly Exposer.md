## Overview

![[Belly Exposer.webp|float-left|400]]The Haradashi is a harmless shapeshifting spirit with a fondness for sake that delights in making others laugh, especially cheering up those who are sad or lonely.

## Behaviour and Haunts

- A Haradashi will appear to someone, often shapeshifted as an ordinary human, and show off their belly which is decorated to look like a face in hopes of making the person happier. This has proven remarkably effective, often filling the target with hopes and dreams.

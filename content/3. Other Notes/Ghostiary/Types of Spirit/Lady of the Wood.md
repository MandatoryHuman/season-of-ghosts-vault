## Overview

![[z_assets/Creatures/Ghost Types/Nang Tani.webp|float-left|300]]These nymph-like beings reside in wild banana trees and a generally benevolent and passive, though they can still be dangerous if provoked. These spirits are most active on full moons

## Behaviour and Haunts

- Nang Tani are known to be helpful spirits, providing food to travellers and especially monks. They are not pacifists, however, and they can be vindictive towards men who wrong women.

### Recognition Guide

- Nang Tani are known to dress in traditional clothing, normally green. She appears as a young woman with dark hair.

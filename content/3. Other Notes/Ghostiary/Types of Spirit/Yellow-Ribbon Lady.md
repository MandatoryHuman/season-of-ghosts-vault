The Ma Phae Wah is a guardian spirit of graveyards.

## Behaviour and Haunts
- While the Ma Phae Wah herself is not dangerous (unless you seek to threaten her or her domain), she does bring a bad omen; at midnight, the Ma Phae Wah may hoist a coffin onto her back and carry it through town until she reaches an unlucky household where she will place the coffin by the doorstep. This is a sign that someone within that household will die.

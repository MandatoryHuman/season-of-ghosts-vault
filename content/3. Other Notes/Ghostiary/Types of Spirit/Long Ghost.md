## Overview
Begu Ganjang are spirits that are commonly called upon by practitioners of dark magic against their enemies.

## Behaviour and Haunts
- When called to haunt a target, the Begu Ganjang does not normally do anything physical to them. Rather, they simply lurk around nearby, creating a sense of unease and fear in the target, and bringing them bad luck.
- In severe cases, this haunting can cause paranoid or psychotic episodes.
- If a person holds the Begu Ganjang's gaze too long, or directly confronts them, it may respond by strangling the target to death.

### Recognition Guide
- As their name suggests, Begu Ganjang can be incredibly tall; in fact, they can grow taller the more a human perceives them.
- Such is their height, that on especially dark or misty nights, a person might see the Begu Ganjang's legs and think they are simply fence posts.

### Associated Hazards
- Misfortune.
- Mental distress.
- Strangulation.

## Exorcism and Resolution
- Normally the easiest way to deal with a Begu Ganjang is to deal with the one who summoned it, or else fight back with a shaman of your own.

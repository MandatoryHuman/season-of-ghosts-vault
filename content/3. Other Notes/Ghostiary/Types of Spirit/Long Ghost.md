- When called to haunt a target, the Begu Ganjang does not normally do anything physical to them. Rather, they simply lurk around nearby, creating a sense of unease and fear in the target, and bringing them bad luck.
- In severe cases, this haunting can cause paranoid or psychotic episodes.
- If a person holds the Begu Ganjang's gaze too long, or directly confronts them, it may respond by strangling the target to death.

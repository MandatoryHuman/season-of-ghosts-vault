## Overview

![[Changed Cat.webp|float-left|400]]The bakeneko is a type of cat-like spirit. Over the years bakeneko have been associated with many different things; from being well-known thieves, to their links to prostitutes, who often use images of bakeneko as a subtle identifier of their profession.

## Behaviour and Haunts

- Most bakeneko are harmless - at worst you'll find a few possessions going missing every now and then.
- Different descriptions of bakeneko include them speaking, possessing humans, raising the dead as zombies, placing curses on mortals, shapeshifting, and dancing to music.
- One time in particular that bakeneko may become more aggressive is in response to a human killing a cat, in which case the bakeneko may choose to extract revenge for his kin.
- Legend says that you can measure the intelligence of a bakeneko by the length of their tails. These same legends say that the smartest bakeneko - those with the longest tails - are prone to developing a taste for eating humans.

### Recognition Guide

- While largely identical to a normal cat when they want to be, bakeneko grow bored easily and are likely to give themselves away in one way or another soon enough.

### Associated Hazards

- Theft
- Possession

## Exorcism and Resolution

- If a bakeneko becomes too troublesome, it can be killed to cause it to reincarnate as a regular housecat. This is an action that's likely to draw the ire of both the cat itself, as well as any other bakeneko who hear about it.

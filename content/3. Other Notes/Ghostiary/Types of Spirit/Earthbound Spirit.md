## Overview
Jibakurei are the spirits of the deceased who passed while still having unfinished business on the material world. While this is similar to many [[Vengeful Ghosts]], Jibakurei are not inherently violent. While one might be bound to a location for the purpose of guarding it from intruders, it's just as likely for a Jibakurei to be bound to the garden they tended in life, looking after the plant life until a new caretaker arrives. Not all Jibakurei are aware that they are dead, and discovering this fact can be highly traumatic for them.

## Behaviour and Haunts
- The behaviour of a Jibakurei is highly dependant on what their unfinished business; generally finding out what it is should be your first goal upon encountering one.

### Recognition Guide
- Jibakurei appear as the emblematic image of a ghost; semi-transparent images of how they appeared in life.

### Associated Hazards
- Varies

## Exorcism and Resolution
- While an exorcism can be effective in banishing a Jibakurei, it is often easier to simply find out what their unfinished business is, and to help them complete it.

## Overview

Tales say that the Hiderigami is a spirit cursed to bring drought wherever they go. Some communities have been known to worship the Hiderigami as a god, offering food and prayer to drive it away, hence its name. Over time, this depiction has shifted, from god, to ghost, to monster.

## Behaviour and Haunts

- The body of the Hiderigami radiates such heat that simply being present in a region can cause drought by preventing rain and drying soil.

### Recognition Guide

- The Hiderigami is most commonly depicted as a woman wearing black, though different descriptions differ, with some saying it looks like a vampire or has a monstrous, non-humanoid form with a single eye atop its head.

### Associated Hazards

- Drought

## Exorcism and Resolution

- Prayer and offerings have been reported to be effective, but it's hard to know to what extent this is survivorship bias.
- Very specific written records claim that if you throw a Hiderigami into a toilet, it will instantly perish.

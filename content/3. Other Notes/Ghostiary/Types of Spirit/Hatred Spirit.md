## Behaviour and Haunts
- Unlike [[Female Ghost|Nǚ guǐ]], Onryō project their rage onto any living person they encounter, rather than just those who wronged them in life. This makes them far more dangerous, and far more of a pressing matter to deal with.

### Associated Hazards
- As well as causing direct death, the vengence of an Onryō can manifest in the form of natural disasters such as earthquakes, storms, famine and more.

## Exorcism and Resolution
- Onryō can be appeased by righting the wrongs that were done to them in life, and by showing them respect and reverence in death.
- The rage of an Onryō is too great for a traditional exorcism; instead a shrine must be built for them in order to deify the spirit and turn them into a [[Kami]].

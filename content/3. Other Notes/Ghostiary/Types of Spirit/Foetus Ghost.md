## Overview

![[z_assets/Creatures/Ghost Types/Foteus Ghost.webp|Float-left|400]]A Toyol is a spirit of a foetus or infant, evoked by a practitioner of dark magics and sealed in glass jars. Many of these masters use Toyol to commit theft or to spy on their enemies.

## Behaviour and Haunts

- A Toyol follows the orders of its master, meaning their behaviour depends on who exactly summoned them.
- Independent of their masters, Toyol are mischievous, loving sweet treats and pranks.
- Generally, Toyol are known to bring bad luck on those who encounter them.

### Recognition Guide

- Toyol appear similar to a normal foetus or infant, though they are often very pale; of course, seeing a pale foetus floating around is unusual enough to tip most people off that something's up.
- Sometimes the spirits appear to have greenish or greyish skin.

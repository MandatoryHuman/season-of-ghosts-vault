## Overview

![[z_assets/Creatures/Ghost Types/Gashadokuro.webp|float-left|400]]The Gashadokuro is a huge being made up of the skulls of those who died on the battlefield, or of starvation. While the souls of these people may pass along or become other types of spirit - especially [[God of Drought|Hiderigami]] - their bodies join to form a single huge skeleton.

## Behaviour and Haunts

- The Gashadokuro is active at night; normally around 2am.
- It is known for killing people by simply picking up and eating them.
- Some Gashadokuro have shown the ability to become invisible, and to show enough cognisance to act stealthily when approaching the living.

### Recognition Guide

- Hardly a subtle being, a Gashadokuro can easily reach 10 metres tall.
- While from a distance it appears to be a singular large skeleton, it is in fact made up of hundreds or thousands of human skulls.
- The approach of a Gashadokuro can be forewarned by the distinctive clattering sound its teeth make; "Gachi Gachi".

### Associated Hazards

- Vore

## Exorcism and Resolution

- The Gashadokuro can be incredibly difficult to combat, owing to its immense strength and durability, alas they are not often open to dialogue, meaning violence is often the only solution.

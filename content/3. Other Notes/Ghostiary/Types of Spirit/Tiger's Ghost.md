## Overview
Ma Trành are the spirits of those killed by tigers and other predators. The ghost becomes bound to the predator as a servant, until either the tiger dies, or finds a replacement.

## Behaviour and Haunts
- After a spirit is bound to a Tiger, they are made to act as a lure, tricking other humans into the tiger's path to be killed and eaten.

### Associated Hazards
- Tigers, or other predators.

## Exorcism and Resolution
- Dealing with these ghosts is simple enough, as you simply need to kill the creature they are bound to

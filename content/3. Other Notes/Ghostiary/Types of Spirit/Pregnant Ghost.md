## Overview

While similar in origin to a [[Maternal Ghost]], the Kuntilanak is far more aggressive and hostile. A Kuntilanak can be created when a woman dies during childbirth.

## Behaviour and Haunts

- The Kuntilanak is most active around the full moon. She takes on the appearance of a beautiful woman to lure in her prey; men, who she then sucks the blood from, dissects, and eats the organs of.
- Some Kuntilanak have been portrayed as a sort of folk hero, similar in ways to a [[Kami]]. These Kuntilanak will seek to protect mothers and those in abusive relationships.
  - Others yet, however, have been known to target mothers and new-borns with their violence.
- When not actively hunting, the spirits are said to hide away within banana trees.

### Recognition Guide

- A Kuntilanak's natural form is that of a horrific monster, however their shape changing nature makes them hard to spot.
- It's said that a dog howling at night may be a sign that a Kuntilanak is nearby.
- Additionally, the spirit itself might announce its own presence with feminine laughter, the sound of a baby's cry, and even the quacking of a duck.
- The presence of a Kuntilanak can be detected by the scent of the [Plumeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plumeria) flower, which precedes their arrival.

### Associated Hazards

- Disembowelment

## Exorcism and Resolution

- Some reports state that a Kuntilanak can be pacified by driving a nail into the nape of their neck, which will transform the spirit into a good wife until such a time as the nail is removed.

![[z_assets/Creatures/Thralls/Liumeng.webp|float-left|400]]Liumeng is a [[Female Ghost|Nǚ guǐ]] who originally passed away due to Gu - a form of dark poisonous curse placed by none but the most deprived of casters.

[[Otonashi Amaya|Amaya]] found Liumeng trapped in the sealed cellar of a ruined house. As Amaya broke the ward on the door and swung it open, a wave of unnatural heat rushed out, carrying a swarm of franticly fluttering white moths. Inside, Liumeng was crouched in the dark, her hands desperately clutching her head. Her skin was inflamed and her blindly glowing eyes darted in a fevered panic. A thick halo of pale moths swarmed around her head.

Maddened by the eternal burning in her veins, Liumeng thrashed blindly toward the doorway, the moths surging with her. Amaya knew she wasn’t attacking out of malice; she was trapped in a hell of endless pain. Amaya held her breath and stepped directly into the swarm, ignoring the burning sting against her skin, and firmly placed her hands over Liumeng’s panicked, clawing fingers.

Channeling purifying magics from a scroll she'd picked up the day before, Amaya drew the Gu curse out of Liumeng’s form, pulling the toxic green energy down into the cold earth beneath the floorboards to neutralise it.

The effect was immediate. The raw, violent red tone faded from Liumeng’s skin, returning to a pale, ghostly hue. The pale green moths dissolved into harmless, floating sparks of light. Liumeng’s desperate grip on her head relaxed. Her blind, glowing eyes finally closed, and with a soft, exhausted sigh, she faded into the cool cellar air, finally granted the sleep she had been so cruelly denied.

The rhythmic, hollow chime of a brass bell echoed across the shrine's perfectly swept courtyard. Amaya readied her spell components as En drifted into the soft glow of the paper lanterns. The spirit hovered above the polished wooden decking. She wore a pristine white burial kimono and a triangular paper headband, her face twisted into a terrifying, bloody grin. Her thick, pitch-black hair cascaded to the floor, ending in a heavy brass bell that dragged behind her legless form.

Spotting Amaya, the spirit’s grin widened. The bell chimed sharply, and thick ropes of hair shot forward, wrapping viciously around Amaya's left arm. The hair was freezing, flooding Amaya with a suffocating wave of despair - a snare designed to trap the living in the ghost's eternal grudge.

Amaya did not panic. Instead, she lunged forward, using the hair's tension to pull herself across the pristine floorboards directly towards the dragging bell. Channeling energy into her right hand, Amaya clamped her fingers over the metal to mute its ringing. "I sever your bonds," she commanded, unleashing a burst of concentrated necromantic decay. The bell let out a muffled shriek, instantly oxidising into brittle, black dust beneath her palm.

Without its anchor, the cursed hair around Amaya's arm instantly crumbled into fine grey ash. En froze. The blood vanished from the spirit's mouth, and her terrifying grin softened into a look of quiet relief. Freed from her karmic loop, En bowed her head and faded peacefully into the warm summer night, leaving the shrine to its tranquil silence.

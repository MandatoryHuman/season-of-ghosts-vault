![[z_assets/Creatures/Thralls/Ruten.webp|float-left|400]]Ruten is a spirit [[Otonashi Amaya|Amaya]] encountered after hearing reports of a spirt lingering around the abandoned [[The Cerulean Teahouse|Cerulean Teahouse]].  As Amaya stepped into the overgrown courtyard of the forgotten estate, her eyes landed upon a murky water garden. She approached and the air grew cold and still.

Ruten floated just above the dark water. His blue robes drifted in an unseen current, and a wide woven hat hid his face. He held an ancient lantern glowing with a pale blue flame. Spectral koi in pearl and red swam through the air around him, leaving soft, glowing trails.

Amaya noticed the faint line of spiritual energy tying Ruten to the pond. He was a [[Earthbound Spirit|Jibakurei]] - a bound spirit keeping a lonely watch over the abandoned garden. He didn't attack, but as Amaya stepped onto the rotted wooden bridge, the phantom koi formed a glowing barrier and Ruten raised his lantern, clearly marking a boundary.

Knowing that forcing her way across would only anger him, Amaya knelt at the edge of the bridge. Using her extrasensory abilities, she realised the problem: a dark, corrupted residue had polluted the groundwater of his pond. Amaya quickly cast a delicate siphoning spell, pulling the necrotic taint out of the water and trapping it in a glass vial. As the darkness cleared, the heavy tension in the courtyard vanished.

Ruten lowered his lantern, and the koi swam playfully toward Amaya. One drifted through her hand, feeling like a splash of cool spring water. Ruten offered a slow bow of gratitude, then turned to light a safe path across the pond's stepping stones. As the final lantern was lit, the spirit faded away to nothingness.

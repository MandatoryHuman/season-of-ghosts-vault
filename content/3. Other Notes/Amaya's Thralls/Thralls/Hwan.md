Dropping anchor where the pale blue glow was strongest, Amaya cast a water-breathing spell from the wand [[Igawa Jubei|Jubei]] sold her and slipped into the freezing depths. She found Hwan suspended just above the dark seabed. Heavy iron chains anchored his wrists, a rotting sack obscured his face, and three glowing spectral arrows pierced his back.

Sensing the living, the Funa-yūrei thrashed. The water churned violently around him as his cursed nature instinctively tried to drag Amaya's boat under. However, she immediately recognised the glowing arrows for what they were: karmic spikes driven into his soul to trap him in a state of eternal, agonised drowning. She anchored her boots in the silt and bypassed his rage, projecting a calm thought into his fractured mind: "Let the anger drown, and I will pull the spikes."

Hwan stilled, the chaotic currents easing. Fighting the blistering, necrotic cold of the magic, Amaya swam behind him and tore the spectral arrows free one by one. With the final spike removed, the heavy iron rusted away in seconds. Freed from his torment, Hwan did not attack. He simply raised a palm which he placed upon Amaya's forehead before peacefully dissolving into the dark tide.

## Backstory

With his mother dying in child birth, and his father passing away during the pregnancy, Jinmada and his brother, [[Shinobura Mamoru]], were taken in by the [[Otonashi Amaya|Otonashi]] family as infants. Through their young life, many of the citizens of Willowshore saw the pair as a bad omen, as evidenced by their disparate fur colours. As a result, many people chose to keep their distance from him.

As a young adult, while exploring the forest, Jinmada met [[Yuri]], a member of Willowshore's ranger corps. Yuri quickly became a close friend to Jinmada and, over the years, trained him in the ways of a ranger. She taught him how to navigate the forests surrounding Willowshore and passed on many of the nuggets of wisdom she had gathered throughout her life.

One day, Jinmada went into the forest to find Yuri. Instead, he discovered her and her squad lying in pools of their own blood after battling a huge millipede. Jinmada rushed to Yuri's side to check on her, but she had already lost too much blood and knew she was dying. With her final breath, she embraced Jinmada before passing into the afterlife.

After Yuri's funeral, rumours that Jinmada was a bad omen only grew stronger. However, after everyone else had left, Governor [[Heh Shan-Bao]] approached him. He knew that Yuri had trained Jinmada and offered him a place in the ranger corps. Jinmada accepted, determined to honour Yuri and keep the memories of his friend alive.

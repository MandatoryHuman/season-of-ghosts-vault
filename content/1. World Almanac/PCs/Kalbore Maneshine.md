## Backstory
As a younger man, Kalbor made a foolish decision which resulted in the death of many innocent people. Riddled with guilt he vowed to travel the world until he had repaid this wrong by saving no less than 106 lives. Eventually, after many years of wandering, this journey brought him to [[Willowshore]], though by this point he was left as little more than a shell of a man. He was no longer a young man, and was unwilling to share much about his history. Despite this, he was taken in by the kindness of the town. The people of Willowshore nursed him back to health and he reluctantly explained that he was on a long pilgrimage of atonement and this was his final stop, possessing only the clothes on his back, an old wedding ring and a necklace.

 Ten years passed and Kalbor fully integrated into the town. He was a different person than the one that arrived originally; he seemed strangely more energetic and youthful, and was filled with stories of his many adventures. Many of these stories seemed like tall tales of dragons and mythical beings, yet the many scars on his body told of another unspoken history. 
 
 Still, villagers couldn't help but notice an aura of despair about Kablor as he failed to find the purpose for which he was lead to Willowshore. The reason for this despair was let slip one day at [[The Leshy's Saloon]] - one of the only places that Kalbore was able to feel respite. He lamented to the owner of the establishment that the necklace he wears around his neck remains a heavy burden; that although the townsfolk have been nothing but welcoming to him, he still feels the crushing guilt of his past weighing on him.
